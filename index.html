Why Tradition Matters

Traditional brewing methods encourage craft and a deeper appreciation for coffee. Each technique highlights different flavor profiles and rituals that modern brewing overlooks.

Whether you're new to specialty coffee or revisiting classic methods, these approaches offer amazing experiences for every coffee lover.

Pour Over

Pour over brewing puts an emphasis on precision and clarity. By controlling water flow, temperature and extraction time, brewers can highlight delicate tasting notes.

Equipment Needed

  • Cone brewer
  • Paper filter
  • Gooseneck kettle
  • Freshly ground coffee

French Press

The infamous French press is known to create a rich, full bodied cup. It steeps coffee grounds directly in hot water before filtering them with a metal mesh plunger. Not only is it one of the most simple brewing methods, but it creates a delicious, robust cup of coffee.

Steps

  1. Add coarse coffee grounds.
  2. Pour hot water over the grounds.
  3. Steep for four minutes.
  4. Press and serve.

Moka Pot

Originating in Italy, the moka pot produces a concentrated, espresso-like brew using steam pressure generated on the stovetop.

What Makes It Unique

  • Bold flavor profile
  • Compact and affordable
  • No electricity required
  • Rich tradition
